Our group asks how individual stem cells divide, differentiate and compete for dominance as human tissues age — and how that same competition, once hijacked, drives malignant transformation. We combine mathematical models of clonal dynamics with multi-omic sequencing of primary human tissue and functional validation in vitro and in vivo. Recent work from the group traces how selection shapes the blood system over a lifetime and identifies the earliest steps of transformation in leukaemia and paediatric brain tumours. Our own methods, LACHESIS and SCIFER, make these dynamics measurable directly from sequencing data.
