Verena is a systems biologist with a strong interest in the dynamics governing somatic evolution. As postdoc with Paresh Vyas in Oxford, she developed SCIFER, a computational tool to quantify drift and selection in human haematopoiesis. Before that, she worked as a postdoc and PhD student with Thomas Höfer in Heidelberg, quantifying the evolutionary dynamics governing malignant transformation and tumour growth in neuroblastoma and glioblastoma.
